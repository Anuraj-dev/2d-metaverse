## Measured performance and lifecycle fixes

- Minimap/FullscreenMap compose static terrain, rooms and labels once per map/DPR change. Ten position updates leave static draw/label measurement count at one and do not reset canvas backing stores. Reactive DPR tracking keeps dots aligned after zoom/display changes.
- Presence refreshes serialize per space with at most one pending follow-up. One hundred triggers during a blocked gather produce the initial and final gather rather than one hundred overlapping Redis/SQL/board passes. Every completed gather emits, so continuous updates do not starve clients; idle tracking entries are deleted.
- Failed stage audience connection releases the dead room so joining can retry. WorldAudio guards pending starts and invalidates generations on stop; stale fetch/connect completions cannot open media after leaving or clear a newer connection. Position subscriptions are replaced/removed correctly.
